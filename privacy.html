Captain of the Ship

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Captain of the Ship (“the game”) uses, and why. The game is made by Captain of the Ship Games, an independent developer based in Egypt.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress (your ships, coins, ranks, upgrades and achievements) and your settings (music and sound volume, vibration) in a file on your phone. This file never leaves your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect, store or sell any personal information about you.

Ads

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional ads you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • basic device information, such as the phone model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with the ads you see

This information is handled by Google under its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, in the Google or Privacy section under Ads. After that, ads may still appear but will be less personalized.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google processes information about your download and your Google account under Google's own privacy policy. We receive only general statistics from Google Play, such as the number of installs, which do not identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Because the game keeps your progress only on your phone, its safety depends on the security of your device. Information collected by AdMob is protected by Google.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy or your privacy, email us at captainofthegame.studio@gmail.com.
