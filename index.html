How it plays

  • Merge to grow your fleet

    Drag a ship onto another ship of the same level and they become one bigger ship. Every ship earns coins, and bigger ships earn far more.

  • Catch the containers

    Containers float through the harbor. Blue ones bring coins or a free ship, gold ones bring a big prize, and rusty ones are best left alone.

  • Life at sea

    Fight off pirates, answer MAYDAY calls with your tugs, ride out storms, and watch for the ghost ship that only sails at night.

  • Sail from port to port

    Reach each port's goal ship to sail on. Every new port pays more, and every rank you earn adds to your income.

Made by a real captain

Captain of the Ship is designed by a Master Mariner who has spent more than 24 years at sea.

The ranks you climb, from Trainee Seaman to Captain, are the real ones used on merchant ships, and every ship you discover comes with a true fact about it.
